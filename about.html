About Me

I am studying BBA in Supply Chain Management at The Hang Seng University of Hong Kong. I am interested in how goods, information, and value move through organisations, and how better decisions at each step can create meaningful impact.

Outside the classroom, I serve as CEO of ARCHIPELAGO · Cloud Ridge House. Building a venture has taught me that leadership is a daily practice: listen carefully, communicate clearly, test ideas, and keep moving when the first plan changes.

This website documents my learning journey, the work I am building, and the questions I want to explore next.
