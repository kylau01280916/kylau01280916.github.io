What I explored

Seeing the chain as a system

The simulation gave me a practical introduction to the bullwhip effect and inventory theory. It showed how local ordering decisions can create wider changes across the chain.

Key lesson: Small changes in demand and ordering can have a large effect across the whole supply chain.
